==================
Perbandingan dengan angka, boolean, dan field bertitik

---

(query
  (and_expression
    left: (comparison
      field: (identifier)
      value: (number))
    right: (comparison
      field: (identifier)
      value: (boolean))))

==================
Prioritas and lebih tinggi dari or
==================

a = 1 or b = 2 and c = 3

---

(query
  (or_expression
    left: (comparison
      field: (identifier)
      value: (number))
    right: (and_expression
      left: (comparison
        field: (identifier)
        value: (number))
      right: (comparison
        field: (identifier)
        value: (number)))))

==================
Pengelompokan dengan tanda kurung
==================

(a = 1 or b = 2) and c = 3

---

(query
  (and_expression
    left: (parenthesized
      (or_expression
        left: (comparison
          field: (identifier)
          value: (number))
        right: (comparison
          field: (identifier)
          value: (number))))
    right: (comparison
      field: (identifier)
      value: (number))))
